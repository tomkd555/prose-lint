# stop-ai-slop-jp register

The phrase lists of stop-ai-slop-jp (https://github.com/iKora128/stop-ai-slop-jp,
MIT, Daichi Nagashima) and the symbol rules of its `SKILL.md`, as regex rows.
`scripts/register.py --slop` reads the tables below. prose-lint runs it on the
`article` and `slides` styles. The source lists stay in
`references/stop-ai-slop-jp/`, and the bracket at the end of each `Why` names
the section a row restates.

Each row was measured on 3.1 MB of human-written Japanese: two technical
books, js-primer v2.0.0 (2020) and promises-book, and 47 Japanese Wikipedia
articles. No blog posts or essays were in it, so a row can still misfire on
the genre this layer reads. The `## ` heading above a row names its group, and
`register.py` prints it with each hit.

- Rewrite, 10 rows: stop-ai-slop-jp rewrites every hit. Each row matched at
  most 2 spans of the human text; 本稿 and 筆者 matched 14, all in Wikipedia
  articles that cite or describe a book.
- Count, 12 rows: ordinary words that become a tell by repetition. The `Why`
  gives the count that calls for a rewrite, and marks a count prose-lint set
  where the source gives none.
- Read in context, 9 rows: forms that human writers also use, such as a colon
  before a label or a 中黒 list in 公用文. Rule each by the sentence.

Copy this file to `~/.prose-lint/slop.md`, or point
`PROSE_LINT_SLOP_REGISTER` at your own. The rules for a good row are those of
`vocabulary.sample.md`.
